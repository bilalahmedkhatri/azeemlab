## 1. Pehle kya karna hai aur kya hasil karna hai

Is mahine ki strategy: **ek useful business problem ko samjhao → apna tareeqa/demonstration dikhao → relevant service par bhejo → chhoti, specific inquiry lo → us inquiry ko scope aur proposal mein badlo.**

Har platform par roz chhay languages mein post karna akele shakhs ke liye sustainable nahi. Pehle apni website aur inquiry flow theek karein, phir LinkedIn aur ek regional community ko customer discovery ke liye use karein. Visual assets ko Pinterest aur video platforms par dobara istemal karein. Har channel ko alag content factory na banayein.

### Pehle mahine ke controllable targets

Yeh **planning targets hain, forecast ya guaranteed results nahi**:

| Kaam | Month 1 target | Done kab mana jayega |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tracking aur inquiry | 1 tested inquiry journey | Mobile se genuine test, receipt confirm, test lead exclude |
| Search foundations | Google, Bing, Yandex ownership/sitemap checklist | Account mein actual state aur date likhi ho; pending ko pending rakhein |
| Service content | Web design aur AI automation ki existing pages ka review | Buyer problem, deliverables, proof aur CTA clear |
| Helpful website resources | 2 existing guides improve, 1 original evidence-based article | Live readable page, relevant internal links; draft ko published na ginen |
| Social copy | 24 scheduled core posts, 4 per language | Calendar IDs ke mutabiq 1 primary placement per post |
| Images | 4 master carousel/checklist designs | Har design se zaroorat ke mutabiq localized variants |
| Videos | 2 short master demos, 1 longer walkthrough | Chhay languages ki script available; selected subtitles publish |
| Community participation | 20 useful, specific replies | Rules ke mutabiq real questions ka jawab; link zaroori nahi |
| Editorial outreach | 8 tailored pitches | Relevant editor/partner, clear idea, no paid placement |
| Learning | 4 weekly review notes | Kis audience ne response diya aur agla change kya hai |

Leads ke liye target rakh sakte hain, magar 30 din mein number promise nahi. New domain/low-data site ko index aur trust banane mein zyada waqt lag sakta hai. Ranking aur AI mention ko kisi deadline par force nahi kiya ja sakta.

### Aaj ke pehle 90 minutes

1. 15 min: website ko phone par khol kar web-design aur automation service samjhein; ek CTA choose karein.
2. 20 min: contact form ka test karein; message ki delivery khud confirm karein.
3. 15 min: tracking sheet banayein; Day 1 baseline likhein.
4. 20 min: LinkedIn profile/Page ki ownership aur website link confirm karein.
5. 20 min: Section 14 ka EN-1 draft apne actual experience ke mutabiq review karein aur ek asli demo screenshot tayyar karein. Calendar ke mutabiq Day 4 ko publish karein.

## 3. Solo workload aur languages ki priority

**Default:** roz 2.5–3 ghantay; heavy website/video days par us din ka content pehle batch karein. Mahine ka takreeban 75–90 ghantay ka planning envelope. Agar sirf 60–90 min milte hain to neeche ka minimum mode use karein.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Language adaptation aur publishing | 25–35 min | Ek post, specific audience, link check |
| Community/relationship | 20 min | Useful reply ya one-to-one contextual introduction |
| Tracking | 10 min | URLs, UTM, time spent, replies, next step |

**Minimum mode:** 3 posts/week; languages ko do hafton mein rotate karein, 1 video/week ki jagah 2/month, 1 guest pitch/week. Six-language copy bank available rahega; sab kuch pehle mahine mein publish karna zaroori nahi. New platforms aur new translated site pages pehle postpone karein.

- `RU`: Russian speakers; Russia ke ilawa doosri jagah bhi audience ho sakti hai. Account access, language aur commercial feasibility alag checks hain.
- `PT-BR`: Portuguese ka initial version Brazil ke liye. Portugal target karna ho to PT-PT vocabulary aur proofreader ke sath variant banayein; dono ko ek samajh kar copy na bhejein.
- `FR`: France-first French; Belgium/Canada/Africa ko ek hi buyer market assume na karein.
- `ES`: neutral Spanish; pehle Spain **ya** ek Latin American country choose karein, dono ke business hours aur examples mix na karein.
- `NL`: Netherlands-first Dutch; Flanders ka context zaroorat par localize karein. Dutch, German nahi hai.
- `EN`: global professional English; pehle UK business-owner segment test karein. Existing site UK/US/global positioning rakhti hai.

Pehle mahine English ko website aur proof ka base banayein; har language ko 4 core posts milenge. Day 14 aur 30 ko actual conversations dekh kar aglay mahine ke top 2 markets choose karein. Yeh equal testing allocation hai, in countries ki demand/ranking ka verified comparison nahi.

**Language review:** neeche wali copy publishable drafts hain, native-speaker certification nahi. Free spellcheck, back-translation aur possible peer review use karein. Technical claims ko source/demo se check karein. Jo language aap bol nahi sakte, us mein live sales call ya fluent support ka wada na karein; written communication aur agreed call language pehle batayein.

## 5. Accounts aur conversion ki tayari

Brand spelling `AzeemLab`, canonical website `https://www.azeemlab.com/`, relevant service link, accurate contact, original logo, real owner identity, service summary aur selected proof URLs save karein. Public footer mein LinkedIn/Instagram/Facebook links maujood hain; ownership aur destination verify karke unhi ko update karein. Sirf existing link se account access proven nahi hoti.

2. Bio mein problem + service + action; Section 14 ke language bios reuse karein.

4. Ek pinned introduction, ek useful checklist aur ek genuine demonstration rakhein.

### Inquiry form aur sales flow

Website URL (agar hai), business/service, problem, timeline, preferred written language aur email kaafi hain. Sensitive customer records ya credentials maangna first contact ka hissa nahi. Budget range optional rakhein; scope ke baghair fixed pricing invent na karein.

Flow: useful post → relevant service/guide → contact → qualification reply → agreed discussion → written scope/proposal. Checklist ki zaroorat ko har dafa contact gate ke peechay na chupayein. Resource publicly accessible ho to sharing aasaan hoti hai.

## 6. SEO aur search-engine discovery

SEO ka matlab website ko search engine ke liye samajhne aur relevant customer ke liye useful banane ka kaam. **Crawl** visit karna, **index** search database mein include hona, aur **rank** kisi query par position milna hai. In teenon ko ek status mat samjhein.

### Current evidence aur pehli priority

Local source mein canonical helper, sitemap, robots aur `llms.txt` pehle se hain. Inhein dubara invent karne ke bajaye deployed responses verify karein:

- `src/lib/site.ts`: preferred `www` origin aur six service metadata mappings.
- `src/app/sitemap.ts`: service, location, portfolio, blog aur image discovery.
- `src/app/robots.ts`: public content allow; API/admin excluded.
- `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`: linked agency content index.
- `src/app/layout.tsx`: English document language; inspected source mein six-language routing/hreflang deployment establish nahi hui.

Repository ki [12 October audit](../../SEO_GEO_AUDIT_2026-10-12.md) historical GSC aur performance evidence rakhti hai. Uske numbers current baseline ke tor par copy mat karein. Day 1 par latest available 28-day period aur comparison period export karein. Is guide ne fresh account rankings, search volumes ya keyword difficulty measure nahi ki.

### Technical checklist: Day 1–3

- [ ] `http`, `https`, `www` aur non-`www` versions se final preferred URL confirm; unnecessary chains note.
- [ ] Homepage, two primary services aur contact actual HTTP 200 aur mobile par useful content dikhayein.
- [ ] Public HTML mein main service text/links available hon; “Loading page…” snippet dekh kar akelay final conclusion na nikalein. Rendered page aur response dono check karein.
- [ ] Important pages par accidental `noindex`, robots block, login wall ya security challenge na ho.
- [ ] Canonical usi final page ko point kare; sitemap mein redirects/404/private URLs na hon.
- [ ] Titles unique, main heading clear, images described, relevant internal links functional hon.
- [ ] Contact journey ka end-to-end test complete ho; analytics test traffic alag ho.
- [ ] PageSpeed mobile report se main loading bottleneck identify; lab score ko real traffic ya ranking na samjhein.

## 7. GEO aur AI answers mein discovery

GEO yahan **Generative Engine Optimization** hai: AI search tools ko AzeemLab ki services aur evidence samajhne layak banana. Google AI features, ChatGPT search, Perplexity aur Copilot ke liye koi guaranteed “submit and rank” button nahi.

### Har important page par

1. Shuru mein direct answer: service kis ke liye hai aur kaunsa problem solve karti hai.
2. Scope, deliverables, process, limits aur contact visible text mein.
3. Real author/reviewer aur substantive update date; sirf freshness dikhane ke liye date change na karein.
4. Genuine walkthrough/screenshots aur source references; result data ho to period, baseline aur method batayein.
5. Consistent brand/contact identity; accurate About/Process pages aur related service links.
6. Organization/Service/Article structured data sirf visible facts describe kare; ratings, fake FAQ results ya AI-only schema invent na karein.

Google ke mutabiq AI features ke liye existing SEO practices relevant hain; extra special markup ki requirement nahi. [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

### Crawler aur content controls

Search access ka decision training access se alag rakhein. `OAI-SearchBot` search discovery se related hai, `GPTBot` training control ka alag agent hai, `ChatGPT-User` user-triggered fetch se related hai. Robots ke sath CDN/WAF behavior bhi check karein. Perplexity ke liye current documented crawler/access guidance follow karein; sirf user-agent naam dekh kar trusted access assume na karein. [OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Perplexity crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers).

Existing `llms.txt` ko readable linked index ke tor par maintain karein. Yeh standard SEO/indexing ka replacement aur AI citation guarantee nahi. Is file par website content se zyada waqt na lagayein.

### Weekly manual GEO sample

Day 2, 14 aur 30 ko same 6 prompts per available AI system test karein: har preferred language mein ek consistent buyer prompt. Prompt translation Section 14 mein hai. Web-search mode, date, locale, response aur cited URL record karein. Brand ko prompt mein force na karein, warna unbiased discovery test nahi rahega.

Saath ek branded accuracy check karein: “What services does AzeemLab offer? Cite the website.” Unbranded mention aur branded correctness ko alag columns mein rakhein. Chhota manual sample total AI visibility score nahi; missing mention ka matlab site blocked hona bhi zaroori nahi. Useful improvement: jis buyer question ka aapki site par clear answer hi nahi, us ka original answer add karein.

### Decision rules

- **Impressions hain, click nahi:** first line, audience relevance, image readability aur destination promise review. “Algorithm ne suppress kiya” pehle explanation na banayein.
- **Clicks hain, inquiry nahi:** mobile form, proof, service clarity aur English destination friction test. Traffic low ho to conversion conclusions hold karein.
- **Inquiries hain, qualified nahi:** post ka customer segment narrow karein; careers/student traffic ko commercial success na samjhein.
- **Relevant conversations hain:** actual wording ko next article/post ke hook mein use karein, person ki private details ke baghair.
- **GSC impressions improve, leads nahi:** query/page intent aur contact journey review; average position akelay goal nahi.
- **Market mein response nahi:** comparable 4 core posts + genuine participation ke baad next-month priority reduce kar sakte hain. Is small sample se country mein demand zero prove nahi hoti.
- **Editor silence:** proposal fit improve, smaller relevant outlet try; mass mail count mat barhayein.
- **Optional platform > 30 min/week setup aur no useful interaction:** pause. Primary buyer conversation ko waqt dein.

CTR = clicks / impressions, magar denominator aur source same hon. Qualified rate = qualified inquiries / actual inquiries. Agar denominator zero ho to `N/A`; tiny sample mein dramatic percentages report na karein. Day 30 par latest **available** 28-day period ko previous equivalent period se compare karein; reporting lag aur newly published page dates note karein. Manual rank searches ko account performance report ka replacement na banayein.
